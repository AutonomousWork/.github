# Building the rails for Autonomous Work

Autonomous Work is an independent collection of projects exploring how AI can help people move through life with greater clarity and agency.

This began with systems I built to thrive while managing concurrent mast cell and endocrine disorders. What started as a way to wrangle that complexity evolved into a personal life operating system, one that helped me regain capacity and accomplish more than my circumstances should have allowed.

The work also draws on more than a decade of designing and delivering enterprise data architectures and cloud platforms, as well as leading forward-deployed engineering teams. The common thread is complexity: learning to make it legible, manageable, and actionable. Whether the context is professional or personal, the principles remain the same.

That experience shaped a simple premise: agentic tools are only as useful as the systems beneath them. Before people can automate effectively, they need solid foundations for managing tasks, administration, conversations, and knowledge.

These projects, including the TACO Stack, establish this foundation through practical building blocks and systems designed to help people level up and achieve more through increasingly autonomous ways of working.

**Organization before automation. Life on rails. Exponential alignment.**
